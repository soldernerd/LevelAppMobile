# Privacy Policy for Leveltronic

**Last updated:** 2026-09-09

Leveltronic ("the app") is a companion app for the Leveltronic precision
level instrument. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data the app collects

**None.** The app does not collect, store off your device, or transmit any
personal or usage data. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or
third‑party data‑collection SDKs.

## Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth Low Energy solely to scan for, connect to, and
exchange measurement data with your own Leveltronic instrument. Measurement
values (angle, temperature, humidity, pressure, battery status) are shown
live on screen and are not recorded or sent anywhere.

On Android 11 and older, the operating system requires the location
permission in order to perform any Bluetooth scan. The app requests this
permission only for that reason and does **not** use it to determine,
collect, or share your location. On Android 12 and newer the app declares
`neverForLocation` and no location permission is requested.

## Data sharing

The app does not share any data with anyone.

## Children

The app is a tool for use with measurement hardware and is not directed at
children.
